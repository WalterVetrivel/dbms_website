About this site

This site helps you learn Database Management System and prepare for your exams.

What you will find here

  • A page for every topic in all five units, written in simple English
  • Interactive diagrams that show how things work, step by step
  • Important questions from the question banks, with model answers for 2-mark questions
  • All 10 lab exercises, with each solution explained
  • Quizzes, key points and revision sheets

How to use this site

Follow these steps for each unit. Select a picture to see it at full size.

  1. Pick a unit on the home page. Each card shows how many topics you have marked as done.

  2. Read its topics in order. The menu on the left lists every topic of the unit. On this page, on the right, takes you to any section or study tool of the topic.

  3. Try each interactive diagram. Change the values and watch what happens.

  4. Read the important questions. Try to answer each one before you open the model answer.

  5. Take the quiz at the end of each topic. After you check your answers, each question says why the answer is right and links to the part of the page to read again.

  6. Before an exam, use the revision sheets and the unit quiz. The 16-mark outlines, diagrams and comparisons help you plan long answers.

Press Ctrl + K or / on any page to search. The search finds topics, pages and comparisons.
