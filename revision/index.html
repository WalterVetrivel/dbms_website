Revision

Each unit has a revision sheet with the key points of all its topics. Read it the day before the exam, or print it on about two A4 pages.

Choose a unit

How to revise with the sheets

  • Read one topic's points, then close your eyes and say them in your own words.
  • If a point does not make sense, click the topic title to open its full page.
  • Then take the unit quiz. It shows the topics where you lost marks, so you know what to read again.
  • For 2-mark answers, use the question bank. Its answers use the same words as the key points.
