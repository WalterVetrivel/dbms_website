Why do we study databases?

Every college, bank and shop keeps data about people, places and things. A database stores this data in an organized way. A database management system (DBMS) is the software that stores the data, finds it quickly and keeps it safe.

  • It gives one clear view of all the data.
  • It avoids unnecessary copies of the same data.
  • It keeps the data correct and secure.
  • It hides the details of how the data is stored on the disk.

Where you see databases every day

Mark sheets

Your marks for every subject are stored in a database. Your mark sheet is printed from it.

Electricity bill

The electricity board stores every meter reading and uses it to work out your bill.

Online shopping

The shop stores its products, prices and stock, and every order you place.

Library

The library stores every book and the name of the person who borrowed it.

Banking

The bank stores every account and every deposit and withdrawal.
