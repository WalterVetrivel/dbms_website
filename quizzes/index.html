Quizzes

Each unit has a 20-question quiz, and each topic has a short quiz at the end of its page. Your best scores are saved on this device.

How to use the quizzes

Take the topic quiz as soon as you finish a topic. Before an exam, take the unit quiz with the timer on. Each unit quiz picks a new set of questions, so take it more than once.

Your progress

Your scores are stored only in this browser. Nobody else can see them.
