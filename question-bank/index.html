How to use the question banks

  • Open a 2-mark question to see its sample answer. Learn the words in bold. They are the key words an examiner looks for.
  • A good 2-mark answer has a short definition and two or three points. Add a small example or table when you can.
  • Each question shows the topics it comes from. Read those topic pages to prepare the 16-mark questions.
  • Use "Print with answers" on a unit page to make a printed copy for revision.
Also on the topic pages

Every question also appears on its topic page, under "Important questions". The answer is the same in both places.
